## 8. Financial and Kenyan regulatory acceptance — technical implementation correct; **owner/accountant sign-off required**

**Implemented policy (single constant `TAX_POLICY` in `packages/shared/src/constants.js`):** 10% service charge (VAT-exclusive per VAT Act s.13(7) conditional), 2% tourism levy (excluded from VAT base), 16% VAT on net (excludes SC + levy), KES, integer minor units, half-up rounding, 15-minute hold TTL, snapshot components stored on the reservation (`subtotalMinor`, `serviceChargeMinor`, `tourismLevyMinor`, `vatMinor`, `cancelFeeMinor`).

**Live verification (staging, quote endpoint):** net 56,000.00 → SC 5,600.00 (10.00% ✓) → levy 1,120.00 (2.00% ✓) → VAT 8,960.00 (16.00% of net ✓) → total 71,680.00; sum reconciles to the minor unit.

**Cancellation fees:** non-refundable plans → full total; refundable in-window → 0; late refundable → `penaltyPercent` × nightly × rooms (documented per plan: `flexible` = 100% up to 48h prior etc. — see `prisma/seed.js` rate plans / `BOOKING_WORKFLOW.md`). Desk settlement and refund paths are manual-record only (no PSP refunds for cash).

**VAT Act s.13(7) conditionality is load-bearing:** the 10% SC is excluded from the VAT base **only if** SC is ≤10% and distributed to staff under a written employer–employee agreement. If the property cannot satisfy that, SC enters the VAT base (+KES 448.00 per 28,000.00 net on the worked example in `TAX_AND_CHARGES.md` §2). This is a **business/legal condition**, not a code tweak.

### Exact matters requiring qualified Kenyan tax/accounting review
1. Written approval of the SC distribution agreement (s.13(7) precondition) or instruction to compute VAT on SC-inclusive base.
2. Tourism Levy base confirmation with Tourism Fund / advisor (net vs inclusive formulas; 2% of net used here consistent with VAT Act s.13(7) exclusion).
3. VAT tax-point treatment of deposits/advance bookings (VAT on receipt vs on supply) — defines when desk settlements create VAT liability.
4. eTIMS registration track (OSCU) — mandatory for real trading; **no eTIMS invoice has been or may be issued until KRA certification** (current mode: `ETIMS_ENVIRONMENT=disabled`; invoices recorded locally with a mock receipt series, never fiscal).
5. Levy filing cadence (10th of following month) and remittance owner; VAT 20th; SC/P.A.Y.E. where applicable.
6. Finance Act 2026 / VAT Act Cap. 476 amendments (effective 1 July 2026 per KRA PDF) — re-verify rates before first real charge.
7. County charges (Nakuru — licence/cess) not modelled; confirm no overlay applies to this property class.
8. Refund accounting policy (cash vs M-Pesa reversal) and whether refunds ever re-open invoices.

**Status: implemented for demonstration and rehearsal; NOT approved for real-money use.** Nothing in this codebase constitutes tax advice. The platform cannot make legal/compliance claims — only your accountant and KRA can.
